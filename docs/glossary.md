# Glossary

Terms used in the code, the UI and the docs. Code identifiers are in English; the Spanish term is
what consultancies in Spain usually say.

| Term                     | Spanish                           | Meaning                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Organization             | Organización                      | The tenant: a consultancy or a company. Owns everything else                                                                                                          |
| Category                 | Categoría                         | A job grade (Senior Developer, PM). Its cost per hour and sell rate live in its rate, never on people                                                                 |
| Small category           | Categoría pequeña                 | A category with 1 or 2 active people: its rate reveals a salary, so it carries a warning (rule j)                                                                     |
| Person                   | Persona                           | Someone on the bench. Has a category and weekly hours, never a salary                                                                                                 |
| Bench                    | Banquillo                         | The organization's active people and how busy they are today, and an open role of each category                                                                       |
| Occupancy                | Ocupación                         | How busy a person is today: the sum of their allocation over chosen scenarios with an assignment covering today. May exceed 100%. Not availability during a project   |
| Project                  | Proyecto                          | A piece of client work with a pricing model. Its price is restricted                                                                                                  |
| Scenario                 | Escenario                         | One candidate team for a project. Projects have several; one can be chosen                                                                                            |
| Chosen scenario          | Escenario elegido                 | The scenario the organization commits to. Its rates and cost settings are frozen when chosen (frozen rates, frozen cost config)                                       |
| Unchoose                 | Quitar la elección                | To end a scenario's choice: its frozen rates and cost config are deleted, and its team can be edited again                                                            |
| Assignment               | Asignación                        | A category (and optionally a person) on a scenario, with allocation, dates and who it reports to                                                                      |
| Open role                | Vacante                           | An assignment with no person yet                                                                                                                                      |
| Project role             | Rol en el proyecto                | What an assignment does on the project (Tech lead). Starts as its category's name; the table calls it "Rol"                                                           |
| Reports to               | Depende de                        | The assignment an assignment answers to in the team tree; none at the top of the team                                                                                 |
| Change                   | Cambio                            | One edit to a scenario's team: add, move, remove or edit an assignment, or restore one (only undo does). Rejected before it reaches the history when it breaks a rule |
| History                  | Historial                         | The changes made in the page, with a cursor that undo and redo move. In memory only; what is left to save is kept next to it                                          |
| Conflict                 | Conflicto                         | A save whose base version is no longer the scenario's current version: someone saved first. Nothing is written                                                        |
| Allocation               | Dedicación                        | Share of a person's working week on an assignment, as an integer percentage from 0 to 100                                                                             |
| Extra cost               | Coste extra                       | Non-staff cost on a scenario: fixed, recurring (per month) or a percentage                                                                                            |
| Fixed price              | Precio cerrado                    | Revenue is the agreed price; changing the team only moves cost                                                                                                        |
| Time and materials (T&M) | T&M                               | Revenue is hours × sell rate; changing the team moves cost and revenue. The price is an optional cap                                                                  |
| Fully loaded annual cost | Coste empresa                     | Gross salary + employer social security + other yearly costs (equipment, training, insurance)                                                                         |
| Net annual hours         | Horas netas anuales               | Hours a person actually works in a year, after holidays, leave and training (about 1,550)                                                                             |
| Cost per hour            | Coste/hora                        | Fully loaded annual cost / net annual hours                                                                                                                           |
| Sell rate                | Tarifa de venta                   | What an hour of a category is billed at in T&M                                                                                                                        |
| Availability factor      | Factor de disponibilidad          | Net annual hours / calendar working hours (about 0.76 to 0.78)                                                                                                        |
| Calendar working hours   | Horas laborables de calendario    | Working days of the national calendar in the reference year × default daily hours. The denominator of the availability factor                                         |
| Reference year           | Año de referencia                 | The year of a scenario's earliest assignment start; its national calendar gives the calendar working hours                                                            |
| Nominal hours            | Horas nominales                   | Calendar hours of an assignment: working days × daily hours × allocation. Used for planning                                                                           |
| Effective hours          | Horas efectivas                   | Nominal hours × availability factor. Used for cost, billing and the effective rate                                                                                    |
| Overhead                 | Overhead                          | Structural cost charged as a percentage of staff cost                                                                                                                 |
| Buffer                   | Buffer                            | Contingency as a percentage of direct cost plus overhead                                                                                                              |
| Direct margin            | Margen directo                    | Revenue − direct cost                                                                                                                                                 |
| Net margin               | Margen neto                       | Revenue − total cost                                                                                                                                                  |
| Margin %                 | Margen %                          | Margin / revenue. Never over cost (that is markup)                                                                                                                    |
| Effective rate           | Tarifa efectiva                   | Revenue / effective hours. The best way to compare scenarios with the same fixed price                                                                                |
| Target margin            | Margen objetivo                   | Net margin % the organization aims for; drives the green, amber and red status                                                                                        |
| Margin status            | Estado del margen                 | Green at or above the target margin, amber below it, red with a negative net margin; without revenue none unless red; none with an undetermined net margin            |
| Result line              | Línea de resultado                | A money amount of the calculation (the staff cost of each category, staff cost, each extra cost, totals, revenue, margins), rounded once to the cent                  |
| Project span             | Duración del proyecto             | First assignment start to last assignment end of one scenario: the dates of a recurring cost without its own, when the project has no planned period                  |
| Planned period           | Periodo previsto                  | The dates a project is expected to run. A reference for new assignments and undated recurring costs, never a limit (v0.3)                                             |
| Undetermined             | Indeterminado                     | A result line that cannot be computed because an input is missing (a rate, a price). Shown with what is missing, never as 0                                           |
| Not applicable           | No aplicable                      | A result line with no meaning for the inputs, such as a margin % with a revenue of 0                                                                                  |
| Partial result           | Resultado parcial                 | A result with at least one undetermined line. Its determined lines are exactly those of the complete result                                                           |
| Over-allocation          | Sobreasignación                   | A person above 100% in a week across chosen scenarios and the one being edited                                                                                        |
| Health check             | Diagnóstico                       | A deterministic rule that flags a structural risk in a team (v0.4)                                                                                                    |
| Rate access              | Acceso a tarifas                  | Permission to see rates, costs and margins, enforced by the database (ADR 0005). Without it, projects and teams only                                                  |
| Tenant context           | Contexto de organización          | The organization a database transaction acts for (`app.organization_id`). Without it, no rows                                                                         |
| Restricted entity        | Entidad restringida               | An entity that holds money (rates, prices, costs). Only readable and writable with rate access                                                                        |
| Summary                  | Resumen                           | The right rail of the scenario page: result lines, margin status and staff cost by category with rate access; team and hours without it                               |
| Open part                | Parte abierta                     | What loading a scenario gives everyone: names, structure, hours and span. No money. Not an open role                                                                  |
| Scenario basis           | Base del escenario                | Everything the page needs besides the team to show and recalculate a scenario. The engine's inputs with rate access, the hours inputs without. Never stored salaries  |
| Access context           | Contexto de acceso                | The organization and rate access a database transaction acts with (`app.organization_id`, `app.rate_access`)                                                          |
| Cost config              | Configuración de costes           | The organization's overhead %, buffer % and target margin %. Restricted; projects can override overhead and buffer                                                    |
| Cost settings            | Parámetros de coste               | The costing engine's input: overhead %, buffer % and the two terms of the availability factor. Not the cost config: no target margin                                  |
| Frozen rate              | Tarifa congelada                  | A category's cost (fully loaded annual cost, or a manual cost per hour) and sell rate, copied into a scenario when it is chosen                                       |
| Frozen cost config       | Configuración de costes congelada | A scenario's overhead %, buffer % and availability factor, copied when it is chosen. Target margin is not frozen                                                      |
| Basis point              | Punto básico                      | A hundredth of a percent (31% = 3,100). How percentages that touch money are held in code (ADR 0010)                                                                  |
| Leaf column              | Columna de hojas                  | Assignments without reports, stacked in one column at the left of the row under the assignment they report to, when there are more than two                           |
| Demo template            | Plantilla de la demo              | The demo organization the seed writes on every deploy. Visitors read it and never edit it                                                                             |
| Visitor's copy           | Copia del visitante               | A copy of the demo template made on a visitor's first edit and found through an anonymous cookie. Deleted after 30 idle days (ADR 0011)                               |
| Member                   | Miembro                           | A signed-in account's place in an organization, with its role. The server finds it before acting for the organization (ADR 0012, v0.3)                                |
| Adopt a copy             | Adoptar la copia                  | A signed-in visitor's explicit action that turns their visitor's copy into a new organization of theirs, out of the clean-up (ADR 0012)                               |
| Sign-up switch           | Interruptor de alta               | A server setting, off unless set, that lets new accounts be created. Off in production until the owner's processing review (ADR 0012)                                 |
| Revision                 | Revisión                          | Proposed for v0.5: a version of a scenario that never changes, which a link or a choice points to (ADR 0013)                                                          |
